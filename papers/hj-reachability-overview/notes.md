The backward reachable SET vs backward reachable TUBE distinction, and where our formulation sits. Ours is a BRT (reach the target at some time within the horizon), but computed by Monte Carlo for a FIXED controller under stochastic noise -- HJ solves a PDE for the OPTIMAL controller under worst-case disturbance, so its tube upper-bounds ours. Also the canonical statement of the dimensionality wall we hit with the eval grid.
